01Record a delivery

If your phone has a slow-motion mode, use it. The real arm-acceleration window is only ~40–60ms — faster than a single frame of standard 30fps video — so slow-mo (120–240fps) gets meaningfully closer to resolving it.

06Not fully measured

Pose tracking (when it loads successfully) covers more than the motion-only pass did, but these still have real gaps:
Front leg block — now measured as knee flexion angle (plant → release) when pose tracking succeeds. Falls back to unmeasured if tracking confidence is too low.
Balance & posture — head lateral drift is tracked as a rough pixel/inch range, but that's a 2D horizontal proxy, not full 3D posture assessment.
Hip-shoulder separation — computable from shoulder/hip keypoint angles, but a side-on camera foreshortens rotation toward the lens badly. Treat any number here as rough unless filmed from an angle that actually shows the turn.
Arm slot — same camera-angle limitation as separation above; a single 2D side view can't fully capture the arm's rotation around its own axis.
